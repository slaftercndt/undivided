## STORY 1 — Releasing Trapped Value  *(Potential)*

Most portfolios rest on a return profile that has quietly stopped working. Capital is trapped
as working capital. Credit risk is priced into every position. Returns are correlated to the
same macro factors everyone else holds — so diversification is nominal, not real.

The deepest inefficiency sits in real-world assets. Real estate, energy, logistics,
agriculture, and infrastructure are among the largest and least liquid asset classes on earth.
They are real, income-producing, structurally sound — yet they remain stuck, because the
administrative layer around them is a mess. Ownership records are incomplete, encumbrances are
unclear, and because of that, serious capital won't touch them at velocity. The asset is sound;
the *paperwork* is the liability.

That stuck value is **potential energy** — stored, waiting, doing no work. This thesis is about
converting it into motion.

## STORY 2 — Risk Removed Through Proof  *(the conversion)*

The reason those assets are mispriced is information asymmetry: you cannot continuously prove
what an asset is, who holds which rights to it, and what it is producing. So the market charges
a premium for that uncertainty — on every credit spread and every illiquidity discount.

Conduit's synchronized-accounting layer changes the underlying state. A single accounting
record projects atomically onto five surfaces — legal, identity, accounting/asset, payment,
and risk — so the books, the asset register, rights transfers, settlements, and the risk state
are always in sync, because they are projections of *one* event rather than separate records
reconciled after the fact. This is **"audit once, prove many":** the logic is certified once,
then runs continuously provable across every transaction.

When state becomes continuously provable, the asymmetry premium has nothing to price. Markets
that have priced asymmetric-information premiums into every credit spread and illiquidity
discount can re-price as the asymmetry closes. **That re-pricing is the return** — and the risk
is *reduced*, not repackaged, because the fund removes the informational cause of the premium
rather than absorbing hidden exposure.

This is the conversion event: proof is how trapped potential becomes motion, without adding risk.

### How it lands on the balance sheet

The re-rated assets arrive as a new, non-correlated asset class. The vehicle is well-understood:
assets held in trust (a Mutualized Asset Trust), monetized through a convertible instrument
(a Computable Asset Bond) that sits inside the existing asset-backed-securities framework.
Nothing legally exotic about the instrument. What is novel is the operational layer beneath it —
synchronized accounting, the digital twin, on-demand tokenization — which makes the bond's
underlying claim continuously verifiable in a way conventional ABS cannot match.

The analog is deliberate: this is what a commercial bank's structured-finance group does —
originate, package into ABS, sell into the bond market — except the "loans" are real-world
assets and the packaging is a continuously-provable trust holding. A holder buys an
income-producing real-world asset, debt-free, trust-held, with a **110% asset-backing floor** —
not a crypto instrument. Uncorrelated to conventional holdings, because value derives from the
asset's own output and the closing of its administrative asymmetry, not from market beta.

### Underwritten the way you already underwrite

Return is modeled on a discounted-cash-flow basis. Each asset's yield is NPV-valued — the
corpus specifies an explicit discount-factor mechanism `[Discount Factor ~48 at 10% growth /
25% risk over 25 years — RE-SOURCE before client use]`. Cost reduction and yield accrue
together, because the same mechanism that removes asymmetry also lowers the cost of
administering the asset. Freed working capital recycles into the next cycle; cycle *velocity* —
not per-cycle margin — is the primary driver of return on capital. A DCF story with a
structural tailwind, not a momentum story.

## STORY 3 — Three Layers Made One  *(Kinetic, sustained)*

A single re-rating is a trade. A *machine* that produces re-ratings repeatedly is a fund. What
makes this repeatable is the integration of three layers into one flow:

- **Layer 0 — the substrate.** Conduit's core services: distributed authority mapped into the
  ledger, computable contracts (a superset of smart contracts that separate rights from
  obligations on a temporal ledger), and reality-mirroring digital twins that record
  encumbrances before tokenization and materialize tradable claims on demand. Described by its
  authors as "an open, network-wide security infrastructure that was missing from the original
  design of the internet" — the reason *Conduit is to commerce what TCP/IP was to the internet.*
- **The capital stack — deployed through the substrate.** The trust/bond vehicle, the DCF
  reward economics, the 110% floor — capital that moves natively through the provable layer.
- **The business-development engine.** Origination of real-world assets into the stack: the
  motion that keeps the flywheel turning.

Undivided operates this integration — a **Layer 1 on Conduit's Layer 0**, with a capital stack
deployed through it. That seam is the innovation and the moat. A competitor cannot reach
continuously-provable state by bolting tokenization onto conventional records; provability has
to be designed in from the substrate up, and the origination engine has to be built alongside it.

*Light anchor:* the 2025 Nobel work on innovation-driven growth framed value captured in opaque
"economic black holes" that stall the loop between capital and productivity. This is one
operational answer — re-opening trapped real-world value by making it legible.
