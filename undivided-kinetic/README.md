## The one-sentence spine

> When the administrative reality of a real-world asset is made continuous, verifiable,
> and computable, the information asymmetry that forces credit-risk and illiquidity
> premiums into pricing collapses. As the asymmetry closes, the asset re-prices —
> and that spread is the return.

Potential (stored, trapped value) → conversion (proof) → Kinetic (value in motion).

## Non-negotiables (carried into every artifact)

1. **Attribution discipline.** Conduit substrate (tech, tokens, trust/bond constructs, PoEA)
   is the IP of Scott Nelson / Michael Hiles / Conduit Network / the Mutualized Societies.
   Undivided's ownable layer is the **fund strategy, synthesis, framing, and application** —
   never the substrate. Cite, don't claim. See `attribution/authorship-map.md`.
2. **Audience-gated language.** Token-forward framing → technical/institutional readers only
   (per the corpus's own brand-voice rule). Conventional-first for allocators. Faith framing
   translated to secular for institutional/capital contexts.
3. **Cite-and-flag on numbers.** No invented market figures. Every unverified figure is
   bracketed `[LIKE THIS]` and must be independently sourced before client-facing use.
   The Discount Factor ~48 NPV mechanism specifically requires re-sourcing.
4. **Not legal/financial advice.** Fund-structure material flags where counsel is required.
