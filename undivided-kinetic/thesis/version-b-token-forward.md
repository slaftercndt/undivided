## STORY 1 — Value Trapped in Economic Black Holes  *(Potential)*

The 2025 Nobel work formalized why innovation-driven growth stalls when IP and productive value
are captured in opaque entities that suppress the feedback loop between propositional knowledge
(science/IP) and prescriptive knowledge (applications). Conduit operationalizes a fix: mine on
contribution, burn on use, hold the substrate in trust — value accrues to the creators and
operators of economic activity rather than to passive rent-holders.

## STORY 2 — Proof of Economic Activity  *(the conversion)*

The conversion engine is **PoEA** — consensus that mines against economic contribution (CROP)
rather than hashing:

- **No mining race.** Each party mines on its own DLT; blocks are individual, validated by
  registry nodes — no network-wide reset, no winner-take-all.
- **GDP-proxy issuance.** Mining triggers on CROP-purchase thresholds attached to real
  transactions, so issuance tracks real economic activity.
- **Radically efficient.** ~1 second to confirm vs Bitcoin's ~10 minutes; ~78 kWh/token vs
  143,000+ (~1,833× less energy) — a real ESG anchor.

The three-token loop:
- **CNDT** — asset-backed IP token; fixed supply 1,006,632,960; PoEA-mined over 60 phases
  (designed to double per phase); burned to pay IP fees → deflationary floor.
- **SWCx** — option/governance token; PoEA-mined; burned for Squares or (with CNDT) to mint MDPN.
- **MDPN** — universal settlement currency; minted only by burning CNDT + SWCx ("Asset Alchemy").

*Terminology: Square minting uses **PoEG** (Proof of Economic Growth, high-water-mark based) —
distinct from PoEA. Preserved, not flattened.*

## STORY 3 — Three Layers Made One  *(Kinetic, sustained)*

The flywheel across three siblings, each with a token and a role:

- **CDM (Value / CNDT)** — the organiser: turns messy real-world assets into clean, verified
  Computable Assets (digital twins) institutions can transact against.
- **MS (Liquidity / MDPN)** — the banker: packages Computable Assets into bonds for pensions,
  insurers, sovereigns — 50 years of securitization practice, applied to real-world assets.
- **MRN (Demand / SWCx)** — the demand engine: brings everyday participants in; rewards (CROP)
  convert to real ownership (Squares); every Square minted burns SWCx.

Loop: CDM creates value → MS creates liquidity → MRN creates demand → pulls more assets into
computable form → back to CDM. Every treasury holds all three tokens, so alignment is
structural, not negotiated.

**Substrate primitives:** distributed authority mapped into the ledger; computable contracts
separating rights and obligations on a temporal ledger; reality-mirroring digital twins
materializing tradable tokens on demand to Layer 1. Core services are a "DLT of DLTs and
blockchains" merging Web2 and Web3 at the hardware/OS level — the operating system for commerce.

## THE FUND STRATEGY — capturing substrate appreciation

Two return modes:
1. **Conventional asset returns** — debt-free, trust-held, 110%-backed real-world-asset yield,
   DCF-underwritten (the whole of Version A).
2. **Substrate-token treasury appreciation** — as network economic throughput compounds, the
   CNDT/SWCx/MDPN treasury appreciates via the burn-mine dynamic. The non-correlated overlay:
   value driven by economic throughput, not market beta.

Undivided structures exposure so Mode 1 underwrites the position conventionally and Mode 2 is
upside — **never the underwriting basis.** This keeps the strategy legible to conventional DD
while retaining the substrate's asymmetric upside.
